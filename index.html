03_about_me.md

About & Philosophy

I am an Honours Mathematics and Physics student, photographer, and boulderer. My photography is rooted in patience and mechanical simplicity: "Date your camera body, marry your prime lenses."

Recognized with the Grand Prize in the Royal Ontario Museum (ROM) Wildlife Photographer of the Year Youth Contest, my work highlights intimate moments with avian predators, marine life, and raw wilderness landscapes without disturbing natural behaviors.

Primary Kit & Field Tools

  • Bodies: Full-frame Digital Workhorse & 35mm Manual Film SLR
  • Glass: 28mm Pancake, 35mm f/1.8, 50mm f/1.4, 105mm Micro
  • Shoes: Soft sticky rubber for technical bouldering footwork
