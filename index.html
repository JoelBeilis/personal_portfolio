Recipes and Munchies

Random things I can't stop eating... or seeing in my dreams

⏱️ 45 min

Seared Tuna

Ingredients

  • tuna steak
  • soy sauce
  • sesame oil
  • minced garlic
  • minced ginger
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate the tuna steak in soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger for as long as you can (20min minimum).
  2. Heat a pan over high heat and sear the tuna steak for 1-2 minutes on each side, keeping the center rare.
  3. Remove from heat and let it rest for a few minutes before slicing.
  4. Serve with your choice of veg and rice.
Asian Rice Dish
⏱️ 1 hr

Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Melt the butter and set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork.
  4. Stir in the melted butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla extract.
  5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour and baking soda.
  6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  8. Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
banana
⏱️ 1 day

Marinated Red Onions

Ingredients

  • 1 red onion
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 Tbsp. salt
  • 1 Tbsp. sugar

Instructions

  1. Slice the red onion thinly and place in a jar.
  2. Warm up some water to lukewarm.
  3. Shake it all up in a jar and shove it in the fridge until tmrw.
